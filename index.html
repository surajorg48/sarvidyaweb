Welcome to Sarvidya College

Sarvidya College of Arts, Science & Commerce has been a beacon of academic excellence since 1985. Affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University and approved by UGC under Section 2(f) and 12(B), we are committed to providing quality education that nurtures intellect, character and innovation.

With our NAAC A+ accreditation and a sprawling eco-friendly campus, Sarvidya offers a holistic environment where over 8,500 students learn, grow and transform into responsible global citizens.
