Right to Information (RTI)

As per the Right to Information Act, 2005, Sarvidya College of Arts, Science & Commerce is committed to providing information to citizens in a transparent and time-bound manner. The following are the details of our Public Information Officers (PIOs).

How to File an RTI Application
1
Write Application
Address to the PIO, Sarvidya College. Mention specific information needed.
2
Pay Fee
Attach Rs.10/- application fee as DD/IPO/Cash in favour of Sarvidya College.
3
Submit Application
Submit in person or by post to Sarvidya College, Ganeshkhind Road, Pune 411007.
4
Receive Response
Information shall be provided within 30 days of receipt of application as per RTI Act.

RTI Quick Info
  • RTI Act came into force on 12 October 2005
  • Application fee: Rs. 10/-
  • Response time: 30 days (48 hrs for life/liberty)
  • Appeal to First Appellate Authority within 30 days
  • Second Appeal to CIC within 90 days
