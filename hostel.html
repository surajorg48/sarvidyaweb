Hostel Accommodation

Home Away from Home

Sarvidya College offers secure, comfortable, and well-furnished separate hostels for boys and girls within the campus premises. The hostels are designed to provide a conducive environment for both studies and overall personality development.

  • Well-Furnished Rooms: Available in double and triple sharing with attached bathrooms.
  • Hygienic Mess: Serving nutritious and varied vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals.
  • 24/7 Wi-Fi & Security: CCTV surveillance, security guards, and high-speed internet access.
  • Recreation Rooms: Common rooms with TV, indoor games, and reading materials.
