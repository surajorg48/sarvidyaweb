About NAAC Accreditation

The National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) is an autonomous body established by UGC to assess and accredit Higher Educational Institutions (HEIs) in India. Sarvidya College proudly holds the NAAC A+ Grade with a CGPA of 3.45, awarded in 2022, valid up to 2027.
