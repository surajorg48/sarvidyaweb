Principal's Message

Dr. Suresh A. Kulkarni
Ph.D, M.Sc.

Welcome to Sarvidya

"Education is not the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think."

Welcome to Sarvidya College of Arts, Science & Commerce. It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to our esteemed institution, which has stood as a pillar of educational excellence since 1985.

At Sarvidya, we are committed to nurturing the potential of every student. Our NAAC A+ accreditation is a testament to our continuous pursuit of quality in teaching, research, and extracurricular activities. We believe in providing an environment that fosters intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and a sense of social responsibility.

As we step into a rapidly changing world driven by technology and globalization, we have equipped our campus with state-of-the-art facilities and our curriculum with industry-relevant programs to ensure our students are future-ready.

Dr. Suresh A. Kulkarni

Principal, Sarvidya College
