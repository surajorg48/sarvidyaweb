About Us

About Sarvidya College

Established in 1985, Sarvidya College of Arts, Science & Commerce is one of Pune's premier educational institutions affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University. The college is recognized under UGC Act 1956 under Sections 2(f) and 12(B).

Over more than three decades, Sarvidya has grown from a small institution to a sprawling campus serving over 8,500 students across 20+ disciplines. Our NAAC A+ accreditation with a CGPA of 3.45 reflects our unwavering commitment to quality education.

We believe education is not just about acquiring knowledge it is about developing character, fostering innovation and building compassionate citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.
