Admissions

Admission Process 2026-27

1
Fill Online Form

Visit sarvidya.edu.in/apply, fill the application form and upload required documents.

2
Pay Application Fee

Pay Rs.500/- (non-refundable) online via UPI, Net Banking or Card.

3
Merit List Publication

Merit list will be published on website. Shortlisted students are called for verification.

4
Document Verification

Bring originals of all documents to the college admission counter for verification.

5
Fee Payment

Pay the first year fee to confirm your admission. Seat is confirmed only after fee payment.

6
Enrolment & ID Card

Collect your college ID card, timetable and report to your department on the joining date.

Documents Required
  • 10th Mark Sheet & Certificate
  • 12th Mark Sheet & Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Migration Certificate (if applicable)
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC)
  • Income Certificate
  • Aadhar Card Copy
  • 4 Passport Size Photographs
